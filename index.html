Litbuy shopping guide

A better shortlist starts with better questions.

Treat Litbuy finds as starting points for research. A title or attractive image cannot tell you everything about fit, construction or the final amount you will pay. Record enough detail to compare similar items consistently.

Start exploring ↗

  1. Choose one category first

    Decide whether you need footwear, a clothing layer or an accessory. Compare items with the same intended use.

  2. Check the exact variant

    Record the selected size, color, material description and listing link. Different variants may have different prices or measurements.

  3. Compare evidence, not just titles

    Review detail photos and measurement charts. Note missing information instead of assuming a material, brand origin or quality level.

  4. Confirm the full cost and terms

    Check delivery charges, any service fees, applicable taxes and return conditions before committing to a purchase.

What belongs in your comparison sheet?

A compact checklist for turning scattered product links into a practical decision.

Product comparison checklist
Field What to record Why it helps
Item & variant Listing title, source link, color and selected size. Keeps comparisons tied to the exact item you intend to order.
Measurements Foot-length guidance or garment measurements, including units. Helps compare fit across different size-label systems.
Materials & details Stated composition, construction, closures and available close-up photos. Separates documented specifications from visual assumptions.
Cost breakdown Item price, currency, delivery, fees and any estimated taxes. Makes the likely delivered cost easier to compare.
Seller & terms Seller identity, return conditions and relevant product provenance. Supports a more informed assessment of the transaction.
Last checked The date you reviewed the listing and any unresolved questions. Flags information that may need another check before checkout.

This is a research framework. Prices, availability and transaction terms must be verified on the relevant listing.

Clear questions. Direct answers.

Before you choose.

Understand what this guide provides, what to check on a listing and how to use product discovery lists more carefully.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list used to organize shopping links and comparison notes. Its usefulness depends on whether the linked listings, sizing information and costs are accurate and current.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page is a category directory and comparison guide. Its shopping links lead to CAFANS; it does not host a downloadable spreadsheet or a live Litbuy inventory feed.

How should I compare sneaker and clothing listings?

Compare the exact product variant, listing photos, size measurements, material description and return terms. For sneakers, check the seller's size chart and foot-length guidance. For clothing, compare garment measurements with an item you already own.

Can a spreadsheet confirm product authenticity?

No. A spreadsheet, brand name or product image does not establish authenticity. Verify the seller, product provenance and available authentication evidence before purchasing branded goods.

Are listed prices the final delivered cost?

Not necessarily. Depending on the transaction, the final cost may include item price, domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping and applicable taxes or duties. Confirm the full amount before payment.

Is this an official Litbuy or brand website?

No. This is an independent discovery guide directing visitors to CAFANS. It is not presented as an official Litbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès website.
